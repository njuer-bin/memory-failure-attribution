# Stop, Pivot, and Escalation Conditions

The Research Director must apply these conditions after major literature audits, annotation pilots, and experiments.

## Stop the current method claim if

- A prior work already implements materially the same intervention, construct, and evaluation in the intended setting.
- The only observed effect is the already-established general cost of query-critical false-drops.
- A role effect disappears after controlling necessity, alternative evidence paths, dependency depth, graph degree/position, and conversation clustering.
- Apparent damage is explained by a changed retrieval candidate set rather than the intended filtering intervention.
- Annotators cannot reliably distinguish role/necessity using operational definitions after one documented revision.
- Effects are not robust to reasonable model/prompt variations and no principled boundary condition explains the variance.
- Independent conversation/trajectory count is insufficient for the planned inference and cannot be expanded responsibly.

## Pivot conditions

A pivot may be proposed when:
- a more specific temporal or query-intent interaction is consistently supported;
- a benchmark artifact exposes a tractable unresolved failure mode;
- poisoning/provenance questions show a clearer gap than deletion-role effects;
- annotation feasibility is poor but an objectively measurable alternative construct exists.

A pivot is a proposal, not an automatic authorization to replace the core research question. Record evidence and alternatives; request human approval before changing the charter.

## Confirmatory experiment stop rules

Before any confirmatory run, specify:
- primary outcome and minimum meaningful effect;
- maximum sample/compute/API budget;
- maximum retries and exclusion rules;
- handling of missing outputs and model failures;
- stopping rule for futility or harm;
- statistical model and multiplicity correction.

Do not stop early merely because a favorable p-value appears. Do not continue expensive runs after the pre-specified futility or budget boundary.

## Mandatory human escalation

Ask the user before:
- changing the central research question;
- replacing primary hypotheses or outcomes after seeing results;
- deleting, overwriting, or relabeling original data;
- launching expensive or large-scale model runs;
- merging to main, submitting, publishing, or contacting third parties.

While waiting, continue unrelated low-risk audits and documentation.
