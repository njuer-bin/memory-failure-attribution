## 5. Interpretation

1. **The dominant failure is status omission, not unsupported distortion.**
   Chained conditions lose the status representation in 42–54% of
   proposition-preserved memories; unsupported distortion is 3/330 (0.9%).
   The V3 claim should be restated as **path-dependent status representation
   loss**, with unsupported distortion as a rare subtype.
2. **Chaining, not budget shrinkage, drives omission.** `rewrite_matched`
   (constant intermediate budgets) equals `recursive` (0.462 vs 0.444,
   paired difference 0.000, CI [−0.167, 0.167]). Intermediate budget shrinkage
   is not the cause.
3. **Call count is not the cause.** `repeated_direct` matches call count without
   chaining and behaves like `direct` (3.7% omission), not like `recursive`
   (44.4%). Re-editing the writer's own output is the destructive step.
4. **Source access protects.** `source_peek` has 0/30 proposition loss and 0/30
   omission; the writer seeing the original dialogue at each rewrite prevents
   both.
5. **Decoding and prompt paraphrase do not change omission materially.**
   `temp1`, `temp2`, `para`, and `path_s2` are statistically indistinguishable
   from `recursive` on omission; their point estimates are within 8 percentage
   points.
6. **The joint outcome is not dominated by proposition loss.** PLR is 10–20%
   in chained conditions and 0% in raw/source_peek/retrieval; omission remains
   large among preserved items. Annotation ambiguity is negligible (1 item).

## 6. Gate verdict

**P3 gate: PASS for status omission; NOT SUPPORTED for a strong unsupported-
distortion claim.**

- Drift remains observable after adjudication: 88/300 preserved items are
  status omissions.
- It is not explained by proposition loss: PLR is similar across conditions
  and the omission contrast survives pairing.
- It is not explained by annotation ambiguity: 1/330 ambiguous.
- Unsupported distortion is too rare (3/330) to support condition-level claims.
  Report it descriptively, with exact counts.

## 7. Limitations

- Synthetic Chinese templates; one writer model (`qwen2.5:7b`); five topics.
- 30 trajectories; no power claim. Paired CIs are estimation intervals.
- Adjudication is model-assisted, not human; original A/B labels are preserved.
- Annotator provenance is unrecorded; report as annotator-unspecified.
- Raw and retrieval are references, not production strategies.
- The deterministic topic-mention diagnostic is not proposition retention.
- P2/P3 are not pooled with v2.x or P1.
