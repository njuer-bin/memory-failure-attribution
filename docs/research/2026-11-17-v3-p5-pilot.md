# V3 P5 Mitigation Pilot Result (2026-11-17)

**Design:** 12 P2 trajectories × 6 writer strategies under one 5-stage chain
(220 → 180 → 140 → 120 → 100). Deterministic diagnostics only; no human
status-fidelity claim. Protocol: `V3_P5_PILOT_PROTOCOL.md`.

**Completeness:** 12/12 trajectories, 72/72 conditions, 0 errors.

## 3. Findings

1. **`status_aware` is the best low-cost mitigation in the pilot.** It raises
   MATCH from 7/12 to 10/12 with only 1.03× the neutral length and no
   abstention inflation. It is the only strategy that improves both epistemic
   and intentional statuses without a budget penalty.
2. **`evidence_anchored` has the highest raw preservation but fails the budget
   gate.** It reaches 10 MATCH + 2 AMBIGUOUS and zero ABSENT, but final
   memories are 1.84× neutral length, above the predeclared 1.5× flag. The
   evidence tags are also frequently wrong (e.g. `依据：Turn 4` for a status
   supported by Turn 1/3), so the anchors are not trustworthy as provenance.
3. **`source_peek` matches `status_aware` at 1.23× length.** Source access is a
   strong mitigation, consistent with P3; it is not a prompt-only fix.
4. **`transition_verification` is only a modest improvement and is
   dimension-unstable.** 9/12 MATCH at 1.02× length; it fixes all four
   epistemic items but fails 2/4 conditional items. The verifier often treats
   the compressed memory as the source of truth and does not restore a dropped
   status.
5. **`unknown_heavy` is a trivial-abstention control, not a mitigation.**
   100% abstention, topic mention 0.58, 6 ABSENT. It suppresses status
   representation rather than preserving it.

## 4. Caveats

- Deterministic cue states; the epistemic cue set does not include `考虑`, so
  some possible/considering memories are scored ABSENT. Human labels are
  required before any mitigation claim.
- n=12, one writer model, one topic pair; pilot only.
- `evidence_anchored` anchors are not verified against the evidence turns.
- `transition_verification` uses one verifier call per stage; a stronger
  verifier or a two-model setup is a separate design.
- Do not pool with P2/P3/P4 or v2.x.

## 5. Gate

Completeness: PASS (12/12, 72/72). Budget: `evidence_anchored` flagged at
1.84× neutral; all other strategies ≤ 1.23×. No human-fidelity gate is
claimed; the pilot is for strategy triage.

## 6. Next

1. Blind-annotate the 72 final memories under v3.2.3 to test whether
   `status_aware` and `evidence_anchored` differ on human status fidelity.
2. If `status_aware` holds, test it in the P2 paired design as a mitigation
   condition (P5 confirmatory).
3. Revise `transition_verification` before any further run: the verifier must
   compare explicit status categories against the original dialogue, not
   paraphrase the memory.
4. Do not adopt `evidence_anchored` without fixing the budget and verifying
   the evidence tags.

**Human ranking.** `source_peek` and `evidence_anchored` lead at 0.92;
`status_aware` and `transition_verification` follow at 0.83; neutral is 0.50.
The deterministic ranking was directionally right, but `evidence_anchored`
loses its apparent advantage once human labels are used and remains 1.84× the
budget with unreliable anchors. `unknown_heavy` is disqualified by 42%
proposition loss: it preserves status among the survivors by deleting the
proposition.

**Residual failure.** `status_aware` still produced one real conditional
collapse (`已拿奖学金，将开始健身`), and `transition_verification` still missed
two conditional statuses. No strategy is a complete fix.

**Practical reading.** `status_aware` is the best low-cost candidate: +33
points faithful and −33 points omission versus neutral at 1.03× length.
`source_peek` is the stronger mitigation if the architecture can expose the
source at rewrite time. `evidence_anchored` needs a budget fix and verified
anchors before reconsideration.
